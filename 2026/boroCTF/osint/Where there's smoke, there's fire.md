# Description

> My dad told me a story about how around the time of Halo 3's release, when he was driving home, he saw a massive plume of smoke in the distance while he was coming off of Butterfly Court onto Turtle Creek. What's even crazier is that that a Google Maps car was right behind him. I wonder if it ever got onto the maps? To this day, he's wondered if it was captured on camera. Do you know the former name of the street where the fire occured?
> 
> Flag Format: boroCTF{Street_Suffix}

# Solution

Halo 3's release date was 25th of September 2007.
Searching `2007 house fire captured on google maps` yields [this post](https://forums.thefirepanel.com/t/that-one-time-google-maps-caught-a-house-fire/8576).
The date mentioned in the post (September 2007) is correct, and the location has the streets named in the challenge's description.
One of the comments has [an image](https://www.hilobrow.com/wp-content/uploads/2010/10/480houseafire.jpg) attached on which you can see a screenshot of Google Street View with the name of the street.
The flag was `boroCTF{Eagle_Point_Dr}`.
